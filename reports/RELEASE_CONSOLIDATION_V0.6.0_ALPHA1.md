# GrapheneDB v0.6.0-alpha.1 consolidation

This commit is a single-snapshot consolidation of the complete experimental GrapheneDB/HypoKosh developer-preview tree onto the current `master` parent.

It replaces the stacked PR dependency for release review and includes:

- the complete embedded GrapheneDB source and durability baseline;
- the bounded HypoKosh runtime and Lyapunov-inspired critic;
- FiberBundle v2, evidence lineage, semantic-verifier boundary and epistemic controller;
- frontier-aware recursive recovery and opt-in opposition research;
- content-addressed compact epistemic receipts so full bundles need not be persisted;
- cross-dataset structural benchmarks and the controlled dialectic intervention benchmark;
- five-minute developer quickstarts, external CMake consumer verification and an exact-head alpha gate.

Generated dated CI logs and duplicated evidence bundles are intentionally excluded from the release snapshot. Curated benchmark and build reports remain under `reports/`.

Release boundary: experimental alpha for research and controlled pilots. It is not enterprise GA or a semantic truth engine. Exact-head cross-platform CI, the downloaded public-data run, long soak and release-security certification remain open gates.
