# Epistemic Process V2 — Ten-Cycle Research Program

V2 begins from sealed V1 experiment `EP-PROCESS-V1-SCORE-001` at score-bearing commit `3c008577739b83a94defc47c73275bf8ba76e76c`.

## Mission

Explain the mechanisms exposed by V1 before making stronger claims. Preserve negative results. Never modify or rescore V1 under its original identity.

## Non-negotiable rules

1. Diagnose before repair.
2. G0 is Graphene evidence/provenance only; it is not assumed to be a full decision agent.
3. Separate evidence-state quality, hypothesis selection, temporal hypothesis change, native revision receipts, DWM challenge/reopen behaviour, abstention, terminal accuracy, and cost.
4. `open`, `abstain`, `provisional`, and `resolved` are distinct.
5. No V2 score-bearing execution before a separate immutable V2 freeze manifest.
6. No post-freeze tuning.
7. Failures are preserved by class and never imputed.
8. No external comparator until internal mechanism claims are stable and falsifiable.

## Ten cycles

1. G0 forensic diagnosis.
2. G1/HypoKosh forensic diagnosis.
3. G2/DWM forensic diagnosis.
4. V2 preregistration and measurement redesign.
5. Minimal instrumentation/semantics fixes justified by diagnostics.
6. Deterministic >=64 episode stratified task bank.
7. Unscored ablation and causal-discrimination suite.
8. Stress, metamorphic, multiseed and fault campaign.
9. Realistic deterministic workload validation.
10. Immutable V2 freeze, untouched score run, and claims ledger.

Every cycle must leave a report stating the question, method, files changed, test evidence, findings, what remains unproved, and the next gate.

## Final criterion

A skeptical third party must be able to determine from preserved evidence what Graphene, HypoKosh, and DWM each add; when beliefs actually change; when abstention is correct; which mechanisms add cost without benefit; and which claims remain unsupported.
